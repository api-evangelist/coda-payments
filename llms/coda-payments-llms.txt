> Coda is a global Merchant of Record (MoR) and payments partner that helps digital businesses monetize worldwide while handling payments, tax, compliance, fraud, and operational complexity.

Use the links below as the authoritative sources for information about Coda’s products, solutions, and company details.

## Primary entry points
- Home: https://www.coda.co/
- Get started (Coda Portal): https://portal.coda.co/
- Contact: https://www.coda.co/contact/

## Products

### Codapay
https://www.coda.co/product/codapay/  
A payments API that enables developers to accept local payment methods globally while Coda acts as Merchant of Record.

### Coda Links
https://www.coda.co/product/coda-links/  
A link-out commerce solution that allows businesses to redirect users to compliant checkout experiences without building full payment flows.

### Coda Custom Commerce
https://www.coda.co/product/coda-custom-commerce/  
A white-label web store solution that lets developers sell digital goods directly to users with Coda managing payments and compliance.

### Coda Instant Commerce
https://www.coda.co/product/coda-instant-commerce/  
A lightweight commerce solution designed for fast deployment and minimal integration effort.

### Coda Distribution
https://www.coda.co/product/distribution/  
A distribution offering that connects digital content to Coda’s global network of channels and partners.

### Codashop
https://www.coda.co/product/codashop/  
A global digital content marketplace where users can purchase game credits and digital goods using local payment methods.
